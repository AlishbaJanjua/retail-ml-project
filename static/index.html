Retail ML & Analytics

Inventory forecast, discount/profit scenarios, and customer RFM segments

Built on the Sample Superstore dataset. All outputs are model estimates for learning and planning — not guaranteed business outcomes. No inventory lead-time or campaign-response data is available.

Inventory / Next-Week Forecast

Forecasts next-week Quantity (units) for stock planning using weekly history. SKU-level forecasting is not reliable with this dataset. Suggested stock includes your safety buffer.

Choose Overall for all products, a Category with Entire category for category-level units, or a specific Sub-Category.

Discount & Profit Analysis

Estimates profit at several discount levels while Sales and other inputs are held constant. Historical association only — these scenarios do not establish causal effects or true demand elasticity.

Customer Segmentation (RFM + KMeans)

Customers are grouped by Recency, Frequency, and Monetary value. This is unsupervised learning — there is no true segment label to predict.

Saved Model Metrics

Held-out / chronological evaluation metrics from training — separate from any single prediction.
